1

Discovery

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden fees?

No, all our plans are transparent with no hidden costs. The setup fee is one-time, and the monthly subscription covers all ongoing services as outlined in your package. Any additional services requested will be quoted separately and only proceed with your approval.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan at any time as your business grows. We'll prorate the difference and seamlessly transition your website to the new service level without any disruption to your online presence.

What if I need a custom solution?

We understand that some businesses have unique requirements. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs. Our team will work with you to create a bespoke solution that addresses your particular challenges and goals.

What's included in website maintenance?

Our maintenance services include security updates, performance monitoring, regular backups, content updates (as per your plan allowance), technical support, and proactive monitoring for potential issues before they impact your website.
