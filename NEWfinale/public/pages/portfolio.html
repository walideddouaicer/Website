Featured Project

Mountain View Retailers

E-Commerce Redesign

125% increase in mobile conversions within 3 months after implementing a responsive, user-focused redesign.

The Challenge

Mountain View Retailers, a leading outdoor equipment company, was facing a significant problem: 70% of their users were accessing their website via mobile devices, but the mobile experience was poor, resulting in high bounce rates and low conversion.

Our Solution

We implemented a complete mobile-first redesign, focusing on:

  • Streamlined product navigation and search functionality
  • Simplified checkout process reducing steps from 5 to 3
  • Optimized image loading reducing page load times by 40%
  • Improved product filtering and sorting capabilities

The Results

125% Increase in mobile conversions
40% Reduction in page load time
62% Decrease in cart abandonment
"The WebWacos team transformed our online presence completely. Not only is our site visually stunning, but the improvement in performance and user experience has directly impacted our bottom line."
— Sarah Johnson, E-Commerce Director at Mountain View Retailers

React Node.js Shopify Tailwind CSS AWS
